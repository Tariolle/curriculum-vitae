\section*{Publications}
\entry{\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40003}{DashVMC: Real-Time Discrete World Model Control in Geometry Dash}}{Sep 2026}{F. Tariolle and F. Yger. PTA workshop, NeurIPS 2026.}
\paperlinks{\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40003}{arXiv: 2609.40003}
  $\cdot$ \href{https://tariolle.github.io/dash-vmc/}{Project page and interactive demo}
  $\cdot$ \href{https://github.com/Tariolle/dash-vmc}{Code}}
Developed a world-model agent for live Geometry Dash control from screen pixels.
The system combines an $8\times8$ FSQ token grid, action-conditioned transformer
dynamics, and a lightweight actor-critic initialized by behavioural cloning and
refined with PPO entirely in frozen-model rollouts.

Trained offline on approximately two hours of recorded gameplay. Policy refinement
improved survival over the cloning baseline on all three evaluated official levels
across three controller seeds and on a held-out layout. Decoder-free deployment
sustains a 60-FPS capture-to-action loop on an RTX 2060.

\entry{\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25663}{Opportunistic Target Selection: Early Directional Commitment for Query-Efficient Black-Box Adversarial Attacks}}{May 2026}{F. Tariolle and F. Yger. CAp 2026 (Conf\'erence sur l'Apprentissage automatique).}
\paperlinks{\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25663}{arXiv: 2605.25663}
  $\cdot$ \href{https://github.com/Tariolle/opportunistic-target-selection}{Code}
  $\cdot$ \href{https://github.com/Tariolle/opportunistic-target-selection/blob/main/poster/poster_beamer.pdf}{Poster}}
Developed a lightweight wrapper for score-based black-box adversarial attacks.
It uses early class-confidence signals to switch from untargeted exploration to a
promising target, reducing class drift without gradient access, architectural
changes, or prior target-class knowledge.

Evaluated across 4,500 ImageNet attack runs on five classifiers. On ResNet-50,
improved attack success by up to 27 percentage points and reduced censored-mean
attack iterations by 43\%. Presented a poster at CAp 2026.
